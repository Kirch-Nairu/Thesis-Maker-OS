# Governance

## Research-state rule

A polished sentence does not upgrade the state of a research decision.

If the model says an outcome is unresolved, the Writer cannot turn “selected tennis-specific measures may include…” into a definitive protocol. The model must be changed first by an authorized research decision.

## Gate ownership

| Gate | Primary owner | Writer may override? |
|---|---|---:|
| Source existence / metadata | Citation Reviewer | No |
| Claim support level | Citation + Peer Reviewer | No |
| Construct identity | Research Reviewer / Human | No |
| Variable operationalization | Research Reviewer / Human | No |
| Instrument selection | Research Reviewer / Human | No |
| Statistical plan | Method/Stats authority | No |
| Language/style | Writer | Yes, within approved meaning |
| Final acceptance | Human research authority | N/A |

## Failure classes

### Evidence drift
A real source is used to support a broader or different construct than it studied.

### Theory drift
Related theory names or constructs are blended because their language is semantically similar.

### Methodology placeholder laundering
Phrases such as “appropriate test,” “may include,” or “depending on adviser approval” are surrounded by confident academic prose and mistaken for a finished protocol.

### Statistical convenience
A familiar statistical test is selected before the data-generating design and measurement scales are fixed.

### Bibliography drift
In-text and reference metadata diverge, references disappear, or author metadata is truncated incorrectly.

### Detector optimization
Text is changed primarily to avoid an AI detector rather than to improve epistemic traceability. This is not an accepted objective.

## Human decision record

When a blocking research question is resolved, record:

- decision;
- authority/person making it;
- date;
- evidence consulted;
- affected variables/instruments/tests;
- superseded alternatives.

M0 provides `authority_notes`; a later milestone should make decisions first-class versioned records.
