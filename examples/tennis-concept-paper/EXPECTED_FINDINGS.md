# Expected bootstrap findings

This anonymized case is designed to fail the M0 research-model gate.

Expected blocking classes include:

- conditioning exposure is named but not operationalized;
- perceived effectiveness has no finalized instrument or scoring rule;
- tennis-specific performance is not resolved into separate outcomes or a justified composite;
- the Achievement Goal Theory link has no evidence ID and does not map cleanly to a measured construct;
- the statistical test is selected only provisionally and its rationale/assumptions are absent.

This is intentional. The purpose of the example is to prove that Thesis Maker OS refuses to treat academically fluent wording as a completed research design.
